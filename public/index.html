Everything you need, nothing you don't.

A focused set of tools that work together and stay out of your way.

  • Calorie tracker

    Log meals with calories, protein, carbs and fats. See what's left against your own daily target.

  • Weekly workout planner

    Build a Monday–Sunday routine, set sets, reps and duration, and tick exercises off as you go.

    Record your own routine, set reminders and mark what you've taken each day.

  • Progress charts

    Weekly calories, weight trend and workout completion in clear, simple charts.

    Pick the look that suits you. GYM BUDS remembers your choice on this device.
